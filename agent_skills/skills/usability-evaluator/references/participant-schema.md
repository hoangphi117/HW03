## E-commerce Experience Scale

| Level | Definition |
|:---|:---|
| **Beginner** | Has shopped online fewer than 5 times; unfamiliar with cart/checkout flows |
| **Intermediate** | Shops online at least monthly; familiar with standard e-commerce patterns |
| **Expert** | Shops online weekly or more; works in tech/e-commerce; can spot UI patterns quickly |
